The League

Badminton, told like an epic.

MSBL turns a local tournament into a season-long story. Six franchises draft their squads at a live auction, then meet over three days of team badminton with a live stream, a full arena and a story behind every rally.

I · Sena

Young and veteran, one team

Every tie has two masters doubles for veteran players beside the singles, mixed and open doubles. Families come to watch both generations play on the same side, which no other league here offers.

II · Katha

A story in every match

Each franchise carries a warrior value from the epics: courage, focus, loyalty. Our content series tells those stories for 111 days, from Dussehra to the final.

III · Sangram

Built like a pro league

A live player auction, a franchise structure with three-season commitments, certified officials, live-streamed matches and a ₹3 lakh prize pool.

Follow the build-up on Instagram @the.msbl

The format (tentative)

Two groups. Four playoff ties. One crown.

Six teams split into two groups of three and play a round robin. The top two from each group enter IPL-style playoffs, so a team that stumbles once still has a road to the final.

Every tie is six matches

Men's and women's singles, mixed and open doubles, and two masters doubles for veteran players, each played as games to 15. No team wins on its young stars alone.

For franchise owners
What you own

A team, a squad and a seat at the table.

  • A founding franchise for three seasons, with first right to renew for season four.
  • Your team, your identity. Name and brand your army within the league's epic theme, with your jersey and colours.
  • Spots for your own sponsors. The league gives every franchise branding spots on its team kit for placing its own sponsors.
  • The live auction. A table for your owner, coach/icon players and two guests, and a ₹1 lakh purse to draft a squad of at least ten, with men, women and masters players, from the player pool.
  • The owner spotlight. An owner introduction reel, a seat at one press conference and your logo across league content.
  • Matchday hospitality. Owner passes for all three match days and the opening and closing ceremonies.
  • Network. Auction night brings franchise owners, partners and Gujarat's business community into one room.
  • Price protection. Founding owners pay the same fee in all three seasons, however the league grows.
